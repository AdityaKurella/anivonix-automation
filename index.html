FAQ

Before you get in touch

What do you need from me to start?

A short description of what should happen, which apps are involved and one or two examples of the data. For a fix, send a sanitized workflow export (with credentials removed) and the error message. Never send passwords or API keys in chat.

Which tools can you connect?

Most often n8n with webhooks, Google Sheets, Gmail, REST APIs, AI model APIs and the WhatsApp Cloud API. For anything else, I'll check its API first and tell you honestly if it's a good fit.

How much does it cost?

Small jobs start at ₹1,500 for one workflow with one trigger and up to three apps. Larger or complex integrations get a fixed quote after scoping, before any work starts.

How long does it take?

The timeline is agreed in the written scope. Small jobs are usually scoped at a few days once access and requirements are clear.

How is the work tested and handed over?

I test with sample data, including missing fields and failed requests. You get the workflow file, setup notes, and a run you can watch on your own account before you pay.

Who hosts the workflow, and is maintenance included?

It runs in your own n8n, on n8n Cloud or self-hosted, so you own it. Ongoing maintenance isn't included in the starter price and can be agreed separately.

Are API and third-party fees included?

No. You pay any costs from the services themselves directly, such as AI API usage, n8n Cloud or hosting, and WhatsApp message fees. I'll explain the likely costs during scoping.
